## Hard checks versus reported limitations

Before reserved access: protocol committed; reviewed development artifacts intact; development feasibility passed; frozen scales/code/input hashes match.

Before analytical final lists: source/target/exposure checks pass; at least ten candidates per role meet the unchanged primary and uncertainty gates; the two ordered lists contain ten unique stable IDs per role and match the exported ranking. Any hard failure blocks completion and is reported without automatic retuning. One-year reserved candidate counts are diagnostics and are not a ten-player feasibility gate.

Report temporal evidence for every development-qualified player: retained 2026 exposure, sample/coverage availability, observed runs/wickets, reserved rating where adequate, and combined-window rank. No exposure is not a zero score. A sign or rank change is descriptive temporal evidence, not a failed forecast. Additional data, recency and cohort changes all affect development-to-final rank differences.

Retained peer matching does not fully establish venue, target or opposition comparability. Do not claim IPL transfer, fully adjusted ability or causal pressure improvement. Keep systematic missing-source limitations and ambiguous name labels visible; identities are never merged by name.
